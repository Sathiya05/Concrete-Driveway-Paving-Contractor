Our story

Built Strong. Paved Right.

StoneCraft Paving started with a simple belief: good paving starts long before the concrete truck arrives. Base preparation, drainage, edge forms and the right mix matter as much as the finish you see.

Today we serve homeowners, property managers and businesses with residential paving, commercial paving, decorative finishes, slabs and resurfacing — always with clear estimates, tidy worksites and a focus on durability.
