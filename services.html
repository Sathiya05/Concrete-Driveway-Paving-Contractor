01 · Driveways

Concrete Driveways

New installations and full replacements engineered for strength, drainage and a clean finish that boosts curb appeal.

A driveway takes full vehicle weight every single day, so we build it for that load. We strip the old surface, excavate to stable soil, lay and compact a crushed aggregate base sized to your ground conditions, set forms to the exact width — including the apron line at the street — and place a 4"–6" slab, adding reinforcement where the ground is prone to movement. Slope is graded away from the garage so water never sits at the door, control joints are cut on a planned pattern, and a broom finish adds grip in rain and winter ice.

02 · Outdoor living

Concrete Patios

Comfortable outdoor entertaining areas with custom shapes and finishes that suit your home and landscape.

A patio is planned around how you actually use it — table and chairs, a grill, a hot tub, steps down to the lawn. We set forms to your chosen shape (square, curved or multi-level), slope the slab gently away from the house so rain never runs back toward the foundation, build a compacted base, pour around 4" (thicker under walls or heavy features), saw joints to keep any cracking neat and hidden by pattern, and finish with broom, smooth, stamped or exposed texture. Sealer options protect color and make seasonal cleaning easier.

03 · Access

Concrete Walkways

Safe, level walkways that connect entries, gardens and sidewalks while complementing your property.

Walkways fail in two predictable ways: they settle and become trip hazards, or they stay flat and hold water. We prevent both — a compacted base under the full width, a gentle cross-fall so rain leaves the surface, a minimum 3 ft width so two people can pass, and edged transitions where the path meets driveways, steps or the street. Finish is chosen for grip: a light broom or textured surface stays walkable in rain and ice, with joints cut at regular intervals so movement happens at the joint, not across your path.

04 · Finishes

Decorative Concrete

Stamped patterns, textured finishes and borders that add character without compromising durability.

Decorative concrete is still concrete — just placed with far more care. Integral color or a broadcast color hardener goes down while the slab is fresh, stamps are pressed inside the correct cure window with a release agent so texture reads clean, and borders or saw-cut accents are laid out to the scale of your space before any work starts. After curing we wash off the release and apply a sealer that deepens color and protects against rain, salt and stains. We plan joint locations along pattern lines wherever possible so they disappear into the design.

05 · Foundations

Concrete Slabs

Level, reliable slabs for garages, sheds, workshops and utility applications — prepared and poured right.

A slab has one job: stay flat and carry load, so the details matter. Subgrade is compacted and checked for level, a vapor barrier goes down where the space requires it, edges are thickened at door lines and load points, and reinforcement mesh or fibres is placed to suit what the floor will hold — vehicles, racking, machinery or a workbench. We screed to a laser-checked tolerance, form a fall toward the door or drainage channel, and cut joints in a planned grid so the slab cracks at the joint rather than across your traffic lane.

06 · Restoration

Driveway Resurfacing

Restore tired, cracked surfaces with professional crack repair and resurfacing that improves appearance and life.

If the slab still sits firm, resurfacing costs a fraction of replacement. We pressure-wash the surface, cut out and fill cracked sections, rout larger cracks so they stop moving, then apply a polymer-modified overlay around ¼"–½" thick that bonds to the existing concrete. The overlay can be broom-finished to match the original look, colored, or stamped for an upgrade. When slabs are heaving, breaking up or the base has failed, we say so plainly and quote replacement instead — the honest answer protects your budget for the next decade, not just this season.
