How we build

Preparation You Can't See Is the Part That Lasts

Most concrete failures are not caused by the concrete — they are caused by what was underneath it. Every StoneCraft job starts below grade: excavation to a stable subsoil, a compacted aggregate base laid to the depth your soil and traffic load need, correct slope away from structures, and reinforcement where the slab must carry weight.

Above grade we place to the planned thickness, finish while the mix is right, cut control joints on a set pattern, and protect the slab through curing. That sequence is why our surfaces stay flat, drain properly and resist edge damage.

  • ✔ Compacted base set for your soil and load
  • ✔ Thickness matched to cars, guests or trucks
  • ✔ Slope & drainage planned before the pour
  • ✔ Control joints cut to manage cracking
  • ✔ Written cure schedule handed over on site

How we work

What Happens After You Request an Estimate

  1. 1

    Site visit & written estimate

    We measure the area, check drainage and access, discuss finishes, and send one itemized price — preparation, concrete, options and all.

  2. 2

    Removal, base & forms

    Old surface out, subgrade graded, compacted base placed to depth, forms set and reinforcement added where the slab must carry load.

  3. 3

    Pour, joints & finish

    Placed to the planned thickness, screeded flat, edged, finished to your chosen texture and jointed to control cracking.

  4. 4

    Cure, clean & walkthrough

    Slab protected, curing schedule given for foot and vehicle traffic, site swept, walkthrough done together before we leave.
